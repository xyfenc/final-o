Organizational Communication Theory

Understanding how communication connects people, departments, and organizational processes.

Representative image of a theorist

Theory Description

Systems Theory explains how different components function together as part of one interconnected system. Within an organization, departments and employees have individual responsibilities, but their actions influence the performance of the entire organization. Communication allows these components to exchange information, coordinate activities, and respond to changes. The theory emphasizes that an organization should be understood as a complete system rather than a collection of unrelated individuals.

Connection to Our Project

Systems Theory relates to Project BDCN because organizational communication connects every part of a working organization. The organizational chart demonstrates how different positions coordinate and share responsibilities. The website itself also presents several connected sections that contribute to one complete project. This shows that effective communication helps maintain organization, cooperation, and a shared understanding of objectives.

Section 04

Company Details

Our purpose, direction, and organizational commitments.

Mission

Project BDCN is committed to building a collaborative organization where every member is heard, trusted, and involved in making decisions. Based on Likert’s System 4 participative management, we aim to use open communication, teamwork, and shared responsibility to help each member grow and to reach our goals together.

Vision

We envision Project BDCN as a model of participative leadership, a community where leaders and members work side by side, decisions are made together, and everyone feels valued and motivated to contribute.

Organization Commitments

  • Effective Communication: Promote clear, respectful, and timely information sharing.
  • Team Collaboration: Encourage participation, cooperation, and shared responsibility.
  • Professional Integrity: Practice honesty, accountability, and respect in every activity.
Section 05

About Us

Get to know the people behind Project BDCN.

Project BDCN is an academic project developed for the subject Organizational Communication at the University of Makati. The project focuses on the importance of communication within an organization, including its structure, responsibilities, and working relationships. Through this website, we present our understanding of organizational communication and demonstrate how different organizational components work together. Our goal is to apply the concepts discussed in class through a structured and informative project.
